# Schedule & look-ahead (basic) (`schedule`)

- **Group:** Field operations
- **Phase:** P2

A basic planning view of upcoming inspections, scopes and due dates.

Kept basic per your comment: a calendar and look-ahead of inspections due, scopes with mandated dates and expiring certificates, with simple weekly work plans. Full Last Planner (constraints, PPC) is available later if a customer asks.

Features:
- Calendar of inspections, mandated completion dates and expiries
- Two- to six-week look-ahead
- Weekly work plan with commitments (optional)
- Calendar export (iCal) and reminders

Interacts with:
- Inspections, ITPs & hold points: due inspections
- Scopes of work (RSW), disciplines & tasks: mandated dates
- Certificates, competency & calibration gate: expiries
- Resources & crews (basic): who is assigned

AIP repo status and plans:
- Built: Schedule page (TASKS §9.3); deferred: reminders, calendar export, filters
